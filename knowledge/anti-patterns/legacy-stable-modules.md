# Stable Legacy Modules

## Problem

Old modules may exhibit:
- high complexity
- outdated patterns
- low readability

But remain operationally stable.

## Key Insight

Complexity alone does not imply risk.

## Stability Indicators

- low churn
- low incident rate
- minimal temporal coupling
- stable ownership

## Severity Adjustment

Reduce priority when:
- module changes rarely
- operational risk is low
